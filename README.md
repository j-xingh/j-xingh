### Hey, I'm Jay.

I'm interested in that moment when a bunch of numbers, parameters, and code start doing something *intelligent*.

That's what pulled me into machine learning. These days, I'm exploring deep learning, training models, experimenting with data, and figuring out how to turn notebooks into things people can actually use.

I don't want to stop at *"the model works on my machine."* I want to understand why it works, where it fails, and what it takes to ship it.

One slightly unusual part of my toolkit: a UX/UI background. I care about the engineering under the hood, but I also notice when the thing someone has to use is unnecessarily painful.

**Current direction:** AI engineering, applied ML, and building useful things with both.
